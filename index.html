What it's actually doing: a set of pattern rules built from the phrases that show up over and over in generic AI drafts — stiff transitions, borrowed intensifiers, vague completeness words, empty hedges. Your text never leaves the page; everything runs in the browser, matches the patterns, and scores density against length.

It isn't a lie detector and it won't give you a verdict to hide behind. Plenty of stiff sentences are entirely human, and plenty of AI drafts read clean. Treat the circles as a second opinion worth arguing with, not a grade.
