Questions

Straight answers

Can Telltale prove a text was written by AI?

No, and no tool honestly can. It shows signals such as stock phrases and rhythm. Use them as a prompt to look closer, never as evidence against a person.

Why can AI detectors be wrong?

Human writers, especially students and non-native English speakers, often write in the plain, even style detectors read as machine-like. That is why Telltale shows its reasons and never a guilty verdict.

Where does my text go?

“Mark it up” and the signal report run entirely in your browser. “Ask the AI editor” and the rewrite coach send your text to an AI service to be analyzed. Only use them with text you are comfortable sharing.

Is the rewrite coach a way to dodge detectors?

No. It helps you make your own draft more specific and personal, and it leaves bracketed gaps for real details only you can add.
